# Gate artwork

`grand-challenge-gate.rgb` is a 256 × 256, tightly packed RGB8 perspective crop of the near gate in Figure 1C by Bahnam et al., *MonoRace: Winning Champion-Level Drone Racing with Robust Monocular AI*. The paper is available under CC BY 4.0. The crop was rectified, resized and mapped onto the race gate faces. The opening is rendered by the simulator's geometry.

- [Paper and license notice](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.15222v1)
- [Source photograph](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.15222v1/Figures/img1/multidrone.png)
- [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

`season2-gate-ink.a8` is a 256 × 256, tightly packed 8-bit ink mask. The A2RL, Advanced Technology Research Council, ASPIRE, and Venture One marks were isolated from the CC BY 4.0 gate crop above. Their placement follows the organizer's Season 2 arena photo, with the A2RL wordmark above and below the opening and the three partner marks on both uprights. The existing procedural panels and LED strips supply the visible gate colors. [Season 2 arena reference](https://a2rl.io/news/45/a2rl-drone-championship-sets-the-pace-for-ai-in-autonomous-fligh)


# Reference quadrotor

The third-person model is a procedural reconstruction of the A2RL × DCL reference airframe from the published 2025 competition photograph and dimensional specifications. Its canopy, carbon arms, LEDs, and five-inch propellers are rendered from the simulated pose. [Reference photograph](https://www.esa.int/media/drone-used-by-tu-delft-s-winning-team) · [Competition hardware dimensions](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/rob.70371)
